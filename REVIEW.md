# REVIEW.md

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` | Relied on naive datetimes and failed the strict 10-minute grace boundary and explicit second truncation. | Test exactly 09:40:00 and 09:40:01 for a 09:30 shift. | Normalize to UTC/IST, truncate to seconds, and compute whole elapsed minutes only when strictly over ten minutes. |
| 2 | `compute_work_hours` | Python `round` uses ties-to-even rather than required decimal half-up rounding. | Use a duration exactly halfway between hundredths of an hour. | Use `Decimal` with `ROUND_HALF_UP`. |
| 3 | `compute_overtime` | Did not properly account for an overnight shift's next-day end, naive/aware values, or the 30-minute minimum. | Exercise a 22:00–06:00 shift and a punch-out 29 vs. 30 minutes after end. | Resolve the end instant from attendance date and shift, then floor minutes and apply the threshold. |
| 4 | `create_employee` | Duplicate protection was check-then-act and not race-safe; required field constraints and epoch-millisecond response were absent. | Send concurrent creates using one `emp_code`, or malformed values. | Enforce a unique MongoDB index, catch duplicate-key errors, validate the contract, and serialize `created_at`. |
| 5 | `list_employees` | `skip` used `page * page_size`, total ignored department filtering, and there was no stable sort or pagination bounds. | Request page 1 or filter by a department and compare total/results. | Use `(page-1)*page_size`, filtered counts, ascending code sort, and constrained parameters. |
| 6 | `punch_in` | Unknown employees caused a crash; timestamp was interpreted as seconds/local time; status/date/derived values lacked rule handling; check-then-insert allowed races. | Unknown code; epoch-ms input; two same-day requests; overnight punch before shift end. | Validate employee and request; convert epoch ms, derive IST attendance day/late, use unique index, return 404/409 appropriately. |
| 7 | `list_attendance` | Loaded and sorted the entire collection in Python; no stable tie-break, filtered total, correct page bounds, or epoch serialization. | Seed many rows and filter; inspect query memory/latency and page boundaries. | Push filter, sort, count and pagination to MongoDB, sort by date/code, and normalize response fields. |
| 8 | Missing endpoints | Punch-out, correction/audit, all analytics and explain routes were unimplemented. | Calls specified in `openapi.yaml` returned 404. | Implemented atomic punch-out/correction and MongoDB aggregation pipelines for each analytics route and explain. |
| 9 | All endpoints | No startup indexes; `/health` returned success without checking MongoDB. | Stop MongoDB and call health, or inspect indexes after startup. | Create idempotent indexes at startup and ping the database in health. |

**Not defects:** keeping calendar dates and shift times as strings matches the data model and avoids inventing time zones. Missing `history` and `half_day` in legacy rows are explicitly treated as empty/false. Analytics consume stored derived values as required rather than recomputing from punches.
